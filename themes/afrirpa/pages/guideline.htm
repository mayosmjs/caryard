Research Submission Rules

1000 Words
  • Submit one research text per registration unless the programme committee grants an exception.
  • The research submission text must not exceed 1000 words and should be written clearly for peer review.
  • Use a concise structure: background, objective, methods, results, and conclusion.
  • Do not include promotional content, unrelated links, or confidential patient-identifying information.
  • The submitted title, author name, affiliation, and email should match the registration details.
  • Accepted submissions may be edited for formatting consistency before inclusion in the programme.

Presentation Preparation

Presenter Guide
  1. Confirm acceptance.
    Check the notification email and confirm your attendance by the stated deadline.
  2. Prepare slides.
    Use widescreen format, readable type, and clear visuals. Avoid crowded slides and unlicensed media.
  3. Disclose interests.
    Include any relevant funding, commercial relationships, or conflicts of interest on your opening slide.
  4. Arrive early.
    Presenters should arrive at the assigned room at least 15 minutes before the session starts.
Presentation duration and upload instructions may vary by session type. Final details will be sent after programme assignment.

Registration & Payment

Before Arrival
  • Registration is considered complete only after payment or approved bank transfer confirmation.
  • Student registrations may require valid student identification at badge pickup.
  • Badge name and affiliation will be printed from your submitted registration details.
  • Gala dinner or special event attendance may be limited and handled on a first-come, first-served basis.
  • Requests for correction should be made before onsite badge printing begins.
