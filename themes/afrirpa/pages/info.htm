Submission Rules

Eligibility
  • Submit abstracts through the official online submission system only.
  • Abstracts must be written in English and received before the announced deadline.
  • Submissions must be original and not previously published or presented at another international meeting before the congress.
  • Choose one presentation type: oral presentation, poster-oral presentation, poster presentation, or Young Scientist Competition.
  • The Scientific Committee makes the final presentation allocation while considering the author's preference.
  • One person may be the presenting author for only one abstract, but may appear as a co-author on multiple submissions.
  • Presenting authors must complete registration and payment by the presenter registration deadline to remain in the official programme.
  • All co-authors should know about and agree to the abstract submission before it is sent.
  • Submitters must disclose conflicts of interest where required in the submission form.

Abstract Format

Requirements
Author Details Presenting author and corresponding contact details, including name, email, department, institution or hospital, city, country, and phone number.
Co-authors Up to 15 co-authors may be included.
Title Maximum 20 words. Symbols should be written as words.
Body Maximum 350 words, excluding title, authors, and keywords.
Keywords Provide 3 to 5 keywords.
Tables and Figures Not permitted in the abstract body.
Young Scientist Materials Optional supporting materials may be uploaded as a PDF, maximum 5 MB.
  1. Purpose or Background.
    State the objective of the study.
  2. Materials and Methods.
    Describe the methodology used.
  3. Results.
    Summarize the main findings.
  4. Conclusions.
    State the conclusions drawn from the study.
Use standard abbreviations, avoid commercial promotion, use generic drug names, and exclude patient names, hospital ID numbers, or other identifying information.

Submission Process

Workflow
  1. Save drafts before the deadline.
    The submission form may allow draft storage until the deadline. Unsubmitted drafts can be removed after the deadline.
  2. Submit when final.
    Use the final submit button only after checking all details. Changes may not be possible after final submission.
  3. Keep the abstract ID.
    A confirmation email should include an abstract ID number for future correspondence.
  4. Follow up if needed.
    Contact the congress secretariat if confirmation is not received within 24 hours.

Submitter Declaration

Agreement
  • The submitter confirms that the previewed abstract is correct and accepts that final submissions cannot be modified.
  • Submission confirms all authors consent to publication through congress platforms and materials.
  • The submitter confirms the content does not infringe privacy, intellectual property, or other third-party rights.
  • The organizers may use, publish, translate, reproduce, distribute, and display submitted content for congress purposes.
  • The presenting author is responsible for receiving status updates and informing co-authors.
  • The presenting author must be registered for the congress.
  • The organizers may remove abstracts that do not comply with requirements.
  • The Scientific Committee may reassign the selected topic if needed.
  • The submitter confirms author consent for processing contact details by the organizers.
