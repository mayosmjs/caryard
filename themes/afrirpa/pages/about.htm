Sub-Themes & Abstract Topics

Select a theme below and submit your abstract.

01

Radiation Protection in Healthcare

Worldwide, radiation is used in medicine more than 10 million times every day — from large urban hospitals to small rural clinics. Full integration of radiation protection into healthcare ensures we do not compromise safety and quality for quantity (Bonn Call for Action 2025).

Abstracts may address justification, optimization, diagnostic reference levels and use of AI in any of the following topics:

Topics

  • Standards, Regulations, QC & QA
  • Diagnostic radiography, fluoroscopy, mammography, dental & CT
  • Radiotherapy & brachytherapy
  • Nuclear medicine & hybrid imaging
  • Children & pregnant women
  • Fluoroscopy-guided interventional & cardiology
  • Unintended & accidental medical exposures
  • Non-Ionizing Radiation (MRI, Ultrasound, Laser)
02

Radiation Protection in Industrial and Other Practices

Radiation technologies are utilised extensively across the continent in industry, agriculture, veterinary medicine, and the military. Their use must be safeguarded and optimised across all practices.

Abstracts may address innovations, new technologies, research and development in any of these practices:

Topics

  • Standards, Directives & Regulations
  • Non-destructive testing, gauging & sterilization
  • Veterinary diagnostic & treatment procedures
  • Aerospace & air/space travel
  • Military applications & security screening
  • Safety & security of radioactive sources
  • Transportation of radioactive material
  • Dosimetry & measurements
03

RADON and Naturally Occurring Radiation (NORM)

RADON is the second leading cause of lung cancer, with exposure occurring both at home and in workplaces. NORM poses a significant challenge in managing human exposure and environmental contamination.

Abstracts may cover any of the following areas on protection and exposure:

Topics

  • Legal aspects, standards & regulations
  • Radon & Thoron in workplaces & wider environment
  • NORM & natural background radiation
  • Use, recovery & re-use of NORM-containing materials
  • Transport of NORM materials
  • Emerging industries or geographical areas with NORM
04

Communication, Stakeholder Involvement and Capacity Building

Effective communication of radiation risks is a well-recognised challenge. Training the next generation of professionals — utilising e-learning and new technologies — is essential for building a robust safety culture. Communication must address stakeholder concerns with honesty, clarity and timeliness.

Abstracts may cover any of these areas or a combination:

Topics

  • Education research & qualification standardization
  • Knowledge management & knowledge transfer
  • Experience from radiation protection societies
  • Stakeholder participation & ethical considerations
  • Risk perception & communication
05

Radiation Protection in Non-Ionizing Radiation Applications

Non-Ionizing Radiation has wide-reaching applications in industry, healthcare and daily life. Adequate protection from exposure to NIR must be considered, evaluated and optimised.

Abstracts may cover measurement, innovation, exposure of the public and workers, protection and regulations, ethics, justification, training and education in:

Topics

  • Standards, Directives & Regulations
  • Electromagnetic & Static Fields: power transmission, 5G, electromobility & radar
  • Optical Radiation: UV, Infrared, LEDs, data transmission, laser & wellness applications
